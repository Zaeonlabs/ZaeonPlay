Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026 · Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zaeonlabs (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in connection with ZaeonPlay (also known as StreamPlugins), an open-source desktop application for OBS Studio.

Summary

ZaeonPlay runs on your computer. We do not operate a cloud backend that stores your stream data. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers in the application.

Information We Do Not Collect

ZaeonPlay does not collect, transmit, or store on our servers:

  • Chat messages, alerts, or stream metrics
  • OBS scenes, sources, or recordings
  • Analytics, telemetry, or crash reports
  • Payment or donation details processed by third-party providers

Information Stored on Your Device

When you use ZaeonPlay, the following may be stored locally on your computer:

  • OAuth tokens for Twitch, YouTube, and Kick (after you click Connect)
  • Application settings (themes, goals, alert preferences, Discord webhook URLs you configure)
  • Publisher credentials (OAuth client ID and secret), if you configure them for development or self-hosting

On Windows, data is typically stored under %APPDATA%\StreamPlugins. Tokens are intended to remain on your device and are used only to call platform APIs on your behalf.

Local Server

ZaeonPlay includes a local server that binds to 127.0.0.1 (localhost) only. It is not designed to accept connections from other devices on your network or from the public internet, except where you explicitly configure optional webhook tunnels for donation integrations.

Third-Party Services

When you connect accounts or configure integrations, your device communicates directly with:

  • Twitch — Privacy Notice
  • YouTube / Google — Google Privacy Policy
  • Kick — Privacy Policy
  • Discord — if you configure webhooks — Privacy Policy
  • Payment providers (e.g. Stripe, PayPal, Ko-fi) — if you configure donation webhooks

OAuth sign-in happens in your browser. Google, Twitch, or Kick may show you their own consent screens. We receive OAuth tokens only on your local machine via the redirect to localhost.

Publisher OAuth Application

Zaeonlabs may register developer applications with Twitch, Google (YouTube), and Kick so that end users can authorize ZaeonPlay without creating their own API keys. When you click Connect, you authorize our OAuth application to access the scopes shown on the platform’s consent screen. We do not receive your platform passwords.

GitHub

Source code and release downloads are hosted on GitHub. Use of GitHub is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement. Downloading releases or viewing this website does not grant us access to your streaming accounts.

Children’s Privacy

ZaeonPlay is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can disconnect platforms in the Settings dock, uninstall the application, or delete the StreamPlugins data folder to remove local tokens and configuration.

Security

We design ZaeonPlay to keep credentials server-side within the local application and not expose OAuth tokens to OBS browser docks. No security measure is perfect; you are responsible for securing your computer and accounts.

International Users

ZaeonPlay is developed by Zaeonlabs. Platform APIs you connect to may process data in other countries according to their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of ZaeonPlay after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
